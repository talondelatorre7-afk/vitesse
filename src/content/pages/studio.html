The Philosophy

Unscripted Momentum

We don't script. We don't fake it.

VITESSE was forged from a mutual obsession with mechanical engineering and raw cinema. We started small shooting a stock Alfa Romeo and scaled our way up to tracking the SF90 XX Spider. Along the way, we realized how soulless and manufactured the automotive media industry had become.

We don't use shot lists. We don't use AI. We drop the cars into the environment and react to them in real time. When you aggressively storyboard a machine, it loses its pulse. We shoot on instinct to capture untamed, natural momentum.

The Setup

No clipboards, no rigid itineraries. We lock in the location, rig up the GMC Canyon pursuit vehicle, and get a feel for the car's presence. We adapt to the lighting and the environment on the fly.

02

The Pursuit

This is where the art happens. James drives the chase truck while Talon operates the rig, reacting to the subject vehicle in real time. We hunt for the right angles, reflections, and raw speed.

03

The Grade

Back at the studio, we process the heavy cinema files. Every clip is hand graded in DaVinci Resolve to pull out the exact emotion and atmosphere we felt on the tarmac.

Hardware Specs

The Arsenal

A-Cam: Canon EOS R5 C

Our primary cinema weapon. Pushing heavy 10 bit XF-AVC files that offer massive dynamic range for aggressive, high contrast color grading.

Pursuit: Tilta Hydra Alien Pro

Hard mounted to our chase vehicle, providing mechanical shock absorption to isolate the camera from road vibration at highway speeds.

Action / B Cam: GoPro Hero 13 Black, Canon 250D

Deployed in high risk zones and tight chassis mounts where traditional cinema glass cannot fit, capturing intense POV and mechanical action. BTS and secondary still photography

Texture: 2000s Digicam

An early 2000s Canon point and shoot utilized intentionally to cut nostalgic, raw, low fi textures into our ultra crisp 4K timelines.

Post: DaVinci Resolve

Where the art is finalized. We push our raw logs through a heavy duty color pipeline, crushing the blacks and retaining highlights for a bespoke cinematic aesthetic.

Base of Operations

Chino, California

Located in the heart of Southern California, we have rapid access to everything from tight Los Angeles city grids to the wide open dust of Johnson Valley. No matter where the shoot takes us, the setup remains uncompromising.
